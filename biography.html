About the founder

Building a career in media production

Steven James Derby Jr. is a media creator whose work spans on-location filming, interviews, post-production, live event coverage, music composition, and emerging AI production tools. He founded Aletheia Vox Quantum Network to develop independent coverage of news, culture, entertainment, and world events, guided by the network's mission: Truth • Voice • Impact.

Through AVQN and its related creative outlets, Steven develops original reporting, interview programming, and multimedia content. His experience combines hands-on camera work with editing, production coordination, storytelling, and the technical skills needed to deliver content for digital audiences.
